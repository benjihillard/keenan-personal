# Digital Museum

This project is a two-part web experience designed to give an artist a personal and intuitive way to showcase their work. The public-facing gallery opens on a grid of artworks distilled to their most abstract form — reduced to pure color and shape — inviting visitors to explore by instinct rather than intention. A hover reveals a glimpse of the piece, and a click opens a dedicated solo gallery page inspired by the quiet atmosphere of art museums: a muted white backdrop keeps all focus on the work itself, while a small byline card sits beside the piece, echoing the understated informational placards found in galleries. Behind the scenes, a private, credential-protected management portal gives the artist full control over their collection — uploading new pieces, reordering the display, editing details, and removing works as their portfolio evolves. Together, the two sides of the project serve a single vision: putting the art first, always.
